What We Do

Consultancy & inspection, under one roof

From selecting the right quarry to loading the final container — we manage the whole journey of your order.

01 — Stone Consultancy

Importing Turkish stone requires in-depth knowledge about the suppliers, factories, quarries, selections, price ranges and other fundamental dimensions of this business in Turkey.

Through our consultancy services, we are responsible for giving accurate information, offering the selection of materials, choosing the right factories and quarries, negotiating prices, carrying out quality inspections and providing detailed reports about your orders and shipments — all while keeping the timeline under control.

We advise on the stone industry and on how your company can take action for its stone needs. We take full responsibility until you sell the materials to a third party or use them to complete a project.

Our wide range of services is rendered to meet your needs in today’s rapidly growing natural stone industry.

02 — Stone Inspection

We offer a complete inspection and consultancy service for our customers — this is primarily our field of expertise.

We have been in this field since 2002. Our worldwide reputation comes from commitment to excellence and customer satisfaction. We carefully detect and solve problems, if there are any, before container loading and shipping.

We guarantee order shipping according to the quality standards.

We know well that problem-solving steps are time-consuming and expensive once the materials arrive at the destination port.

That is why our company’s primary policy is to provide shipping of adequate materials matching your orders.
