Our Story

The journey of our marble

Every slab that leaves our facility carries the same story — a story that begins deep in the marble mountains of Anatolia.

01

Quarried from the mountains

Our stone is extracted from the marble-rich mountains of Anatolia. Massive blocks are carefully cut from the bedrock, each one selected for its colour, structure and purity.

02

Shaped in our facilities

The blocks travel to our processing plants, where they are sawn into slabs and tiles, then honed, polished, brushed or tumbled by experienced craftsmen.

03

Inspected, slab by slab

Before a single container is loaded, every slab is checked against your order. We detect and solve problems before shipping — that is our guarantee.

04

Delivered to the world

From Australia to the USA, from Germany to Singapore — our stones are packed, shipped and delivered to projects in more than 25 countries.

Who We Are

Vision & Mission

Our Vision

Our vision is to sustain our position in the Turkish stone market by emphasizing our quality in production, supply, inspection and consultancy services, alongside reliability. We give high importance to transparency; therefore we share our in-depth knowledge with everyone who is — and will be — in this business.

Our Mission

Our mission is to understand and meet the requirements of the market and serve our customers in the best way. Our aim is to provide the finest selection, inspection and consultancy services that will build and strengthen long-term, reliable relationships with our customers and partners.
